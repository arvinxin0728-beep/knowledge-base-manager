# Role Enablement

Use this method to transform evidence-backed product or operational knowledge into role-specific, executable knowledge. Keep organization taxonomies, product facts, internal procedures, and delivery destinations in the researcher instance.

## Role model

For each role, record its responsibility, decisions, recurring tasks, prerequisites, allowed actions, prohibited actions, escalation boundary, success measures, and evidence sources. Do not assume that one role's permissions or depth apply to another.

## Product knowledge card

Include the user problem, applicable role, product/module name, supported version or freshness date, prerequisites, core workflow, boundaries, common questions, evidence, and unresolved verification. Do not present roadmap items or unverified behavior as current capability.

## Operations SOP

Define purpose, applicable role, trigger, prerequisites, ordered steps, expected result, verification method, exception branches, rollback/escalation, risk boundary, owner, and revision date. A step must be observable and testable; avoid vague instructions such as “handle as appropriate.”

## Training module

Define the learner role, learning objectives, prerequisite knowledge, scenario, concepts, demonstration, practice task, answer/check criteria, common failure modes, and post-training assessment. Separate reusable method from organization-specific examples.

## Quality boundary

- Attribute product facts to a current source and version/freshness date.
- Keep customer and employee information out unless explicitly authorized and appropriately protected.
- Mark role permissions and escalation limits.
- Require a testable outcome for every SOP and learning objective.
- Do not sync to an authoritative enterprise store until the connector confirms the write and exact target.
